I design AI feedback systems for engaging with data.My work focuses on where AI can help people move through the hard parts: the analysis, the narrative, the gap between having data and saying something with it. I use personal data because it lowers the barrier to working with data. I've also done research on AI support for [coaching data storytelling](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3793706), [informal STEM education](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3544548.3581567),accessible [self-tracking technology design](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qpG_S5FtgJv1dkzLEUrVTeZcqwa6MRTQ/view?usp=sharing), and co-designing [3D-printed tactile maps](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3663548.3688537) with blind and low vision students.

Keywords: 
Data storytelling, AI Feedback Systems, Personal Data, Mixed-methods, Accessibility
