I design AI feedback systems for [coaching data storytelling](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3793706),  a skill that matters in the workforce but rarely gets taught at scale.

My work focuses on where agents can help people move through the hard parts: the analysis, the narrative, the gap between having data and saying something with it. I use personal data because it lowers the barrier to working with data.

I've also done research on [informal STEM education](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3544548.3581567), [first-person methods](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qpG_S5FtgJv1dkzLEUrVTeZcqwa6MRTQ/view?usp=sharing), including designing technology for my own use to improve accessibility.

Other work includes co-designing [3D printed tactile maps] with blind and low vision students.

Keywords: 
Data storytelling, AI Feedback Systems, Personal Data, Mixed-methods, Accessibility
