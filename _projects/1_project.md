Story Studio is an AI-assisted data storytelling prototype that explores how students can get feedback as they turn an analysis into a story. We wanted to understand when students get stuck, what kind of feedback helps them move forward, and how much control they want over when that feedback appears.

## How we studied it

We observed a data storytelling course and interviewed students and educators to understand where feedback was needed. We then used participatory design workshops to explore how Story Studio could deliver that feedback. We compared feedback students could request with feedback that appeared automatically, and guidance on their process with feedback on the story they had produced.

## What we learned

Participants rated on-demand and process feedback highly for effectiveness. They rated automatic and outcome-focused feedback slightly higher for persuasiveness. These responses suggest a reason to offer more than one feedback mode. A student planning a story may want to ask for help with a specific decision, while a student reviewing a draft may want feedback on how the story comes across.
